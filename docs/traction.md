# Traction evidence

Prepared October 6, 2026. No independently verified participant or outcome entry was supplied to this build session. This is not a claim that the project has no users; it means this document cannot substantiate a traction number yet.

Synthetic seed accounts, test workers, mocked providers, testnet receipts and site visitors are not paying customers or activated reviewers. Record only real consenting participants, label Arc testnet/no real funds, and keep employee identities, raw payroll files and wallet mappings out of this repository.

For each approved evidence entry, record: anonymized company reference, consent date and permitted use, participant/reviewer count, SIMULATION or authorized LIVE_TESTNET mode, period, measured baseline, observed results, support effort and evidence reference. A testimonial requires explicit permission and the participant's actual words. Business outcomes need observed before/after measurements; do not infer savings from a demo.

The first-100-reviewer target and prospective 3–5 design partners in the roadmap are planning goals. Commercial discovery, outreach, testimonials and spending remain separate work; no messages were sent by this build.
