# RD.3 - Interview script and consent draft

Internal discovery material for US firms that pay remote contractors. Draft only; this document does not authorise outreach or a pilot. Allow about 20 minutes. Learn from the participant's current process before discussing ideas.

## Interviewer preparation

Before scheduling, fill in the consent placeholders below and have the consent draft reviewed. Agree who can access notes, where they are stored, how long they are kept and how withdrawal is handled. Do not start an interview with those details unresolved.

Use a participant reference in research notes. Keep any necessary business contact and consent record separately, with restricted access. Do not put interview notes or recordings in this repository, marketing analytics or AI tools. Do not request files, screen sharing or access to the participant's systems. If sensitive information is offered, interrupt gently and ask for a general description instead; stop any recording and remove the sensitive material before continuing.

## Opening (about 2 minutes)

"Thank you for speaking with us. We are researching how US firms review and pay remote contractors. We would like to understand what you do today, including what works well and what is difficult. There are no right answers, and this is not a sales commitment.

"Please describe the process without sharing anyone's name, pay rate, salary, invoice amount, bank or wallet details, credentials or payment records. We do not need payroll files or documents. You can skip any question or end the conversation at any time.

"Before we begin, let's go through the consent choices, including whether you want notes only or an optional audio recording."

Read the consent draft, confirm the participant's choices and record consent before asking discovery questions. Audio recording stays off unless separately agreed. Notes only is a complete participation option.

## Discovery questions (about 15 minutes)

Ask these in order where useful. Follow the participant's words; use neutral prompts such as "What happened next?" and "Could you describe that without identifying details?" Do not suggest that a problem exists or that Kleeno solves it.

1. **Your role:** What part do you play in reviewing or paying remote contractors? Roughly how many contractors does your team handle, and how often do you pay them? A range is enough.
2. **A recent cycle:** Thinking of a recent payment cycle, could you walk us through the steps from receiving work records or invoices to confirming payment? Please leave out amounts and identifying details.
3. **Sources and tools:** What information and tools do you use at each step? How does information move between spreadsheets, accounting tools and your payment provider?
4. **Checking the inputs:** How do you check that work records or invoices are complete and match the agreed terms? What happens when something is missing or does not match?
5. **Changes and exceptions:** How do you handle a disputed invoice, a duplicate entry, a change in terms or another exception? Who decides what happens next, and how is that decision recorded?
6. **Approval:** Who reviews the proposed payment, who approves it and who releases it? What happens if information changes after approval or an approver is unavailable?
7. **Reconciliation:** How do you confirm that what was approved matches what your provider sent and what your records show? What do you do when the status is unclear or a payment fails?
8. **Effort and friction:** Which steps, if any, take the most effort or cause delays? Roughly how much time do they take in a typical cycle? What parts work well enough that you would keep them?
9. **Corrections and evidence:** When someone questions a payment or you need to correct it, how do you find the original inputs, approval and payment status? What is easy or difficult about that process?
10. **Priorities and adoption:** What, if anything, would you change first? Have you tried changing this process before? What would your team need to see, and who would be involved, before trying a different review tool alongside your current provider?

## Close (about 3 minutes)

"Let me check that I understood: [briefly repeat their process and priorities without sensitive details]. What did I miss or misunderstand?

"Thank you. Your feedback will help us decide what to investigate and build. It does not mean we will deliver a particular feature or timeline. Would you like us to contact you once about a follow-up conversation? Saying no does not affect how we use the feedback you have already agreed to share."

Record the follow-up choice separately from research participation. Remind the participant how to withdraw. Do not request a testimonial, a payroll file, product access or a pilot commitment. If asked about Kleeno, describe it as a project being developed for payroll review beside an existing provider. Any demonstration must identify verified behaviour and planned work separately, use synthetic data and explain that SIMULATION cannot pay and any payment demonstration is Arc testnet only, with no real funds.

## Consent text - draft, needs legal review

This is a draft research consent form, not legal advice. The placeholders and handling arrangements need confirmation and legal review before use.

**Research organiser:** [name and organisation]  
**Contact for questions or withdrawal:** [contact method]  
**Participant reference and interview date:** [reference, date]  
**Approved storage location and people with access:** [location and named roles]  
**Deletion date for notes, audio and any transcript:** [date]  
**Deletion date for separate contact and consent records:** [date]  
**Backup deletion arrangements and any limits:** [reviewed explanation]

"We invite you to a conversation about how your firm reviews and pays remote contractors. Taking part is voluntary. You can decline, skip questions, pause or stop without giving a reason.

"With your permission, we will take notes about your role, general workflow, tools, approximate team size, effort, difficulties and priorities. We will also record your consent choices and, only if you agree to follow-up, the business contact detail you choose to provide. Notes use a participant reference; contact details are kept separately.

"Audio recording is optional and starts only with your separate permission. If you agree, the audio may be transcribed by the authorised research team described above. We will not record video or your screen. Your voice and anything you say may identify you, so a recording is not anonymous. You can ask us to stop recording at any time and continue with notes only.

"We do not collect payment data. Do not share payroll or salary files, pay rates or amounts, invoices, payment records, bank or wallet details, credentials, worker names or other identifying worker information. General descriptions are enough. If any of this is shared accidentally, we will stop and remove it from the research material.

"The authorised research team will use the material to understand current workflows, compare themes across interviews and decide product priorities. We will not upload it to AI tools, analytics or this project's code repository. We will not publish recordings, identify you or your firm, or use your words as a testimonial without separate written permission. Internal summaries will omit identifying details.

"You may withdraw your permission during the interview or later by contacting us using the contact above and your participant reference or interview date. We will stop using your identifiable research material and delete it under the arrangements stated above. Themes already combined into a summary that cannot be linked back to you may not be removable. We will explain any backup limits before you agree; we do not promise immediate deletion from every backup.

"This conversation does not enrol you in a product trial, purchase or mailing list. It includes no promise about product features, availability, accuracy, legal compliance or delivery dates. Any future pilot would need a separate agreement."

Choose each separately; do not preselect answers:

- Research participation and notes: **Agree / Do not agree**
- Optional audio recording and team transcription: **Agree / Do not agree**
- One follow-up contact about this research: **Agree / Do not agree**

Record the choices, date, participant reference and interviewer reference. If participation is declined, end the interview; do not record research answers. If audio is declined, keep recording off.
